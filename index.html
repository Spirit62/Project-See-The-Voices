who we are

We got tired of walking past people who couldn't be heard.

We are a group of students who are passionate about inclusion and accessibility. Every day, countless individuals navigate a world that wasn't built with them in mind. We saw the barriers—both visible and invisible—that prevented people from being heard, understood, and truly included in our communities.

Project See The Voices is student-led, focused on teaching and spreading awareness about sign language and deaf culture. We started this initiative because we believed that communication shouldn't be a privilege. Language is a human right, and every person deserves to be part of the conversation, no matter how they choose to communicate.

What began as a simple idea quickly grew into something powerful. We began organizing workshops, hosting community events, and creating resources to help people learn sign language and understand the experiences of deaf and hard of hearing individuals. Through these efforts, we discovered that education about accessibility benefits everyone—it builds empathy, breaks down stereotypes, and creates spaces where diverse voices are not just tolerated, but celebrated.

Our mission extends beyond teaching signs. We want to shift perspectives. We want to challenge the notion that accessibility is an afterthought or a burden. We believe it's an opportunity—an opportunity to build a more inclusive world, to listen to voices that have been marginalized, and to recognize the incredible resilience and talent within the deaf and hard of hearing community.

Today, we continue this work with renewed purpose. We're not just creating awareness; we're creating change. One conversation, one workshop, one moment of genuine connection at a time.
